Antibiotic resistance

01

Antibiotics treat bacteria

They can treat some bacterial infections, but not viral infections such as most colds and flu.

02

Resistance is in germs

Bacteria can change over time so medicines may no longer work effectively against them.

03

Everyone has a role

Infection prevention and responsible medicine use help protect antibiotic effectiveness.
